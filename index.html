♿ A11y Snapshot

Paste your page's HTML source and get a real automated accessibility report — powered by axe-core, the same open-source engine behind most professional accessibility audits. Runs entirely in a sandboxed iframe in your own browser; your markup is never sent anywhere.

Paste your HTML

Right-click your page → View Page Source → select all → copy, then paste below. (Not the same as "Inspect" — that shows the live DOM after scripts run, which works too, but raw source is easiest to grab.)

What this catches — and what it doesn't

Automated tools like axe-core reliably catch roughly a third to half of real WCAG issues — missing alt text, unlabeled form fields, insufficient color contrast, missing document language, broken heading structure, invalid ARIA usage. Real strengths, real limits.

What it can't judge: whether your alt text actually describes the image, whether your reading order makes sense, whether a keyboard-only user can actually complete your checkout flow. A clean scan here is a real, useful signal — not a legal guarantee of compliance, and not a substitute for testing with a screen reader or a real user.
